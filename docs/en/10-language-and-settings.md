# 10. Settings: Gemini key and Health Connect

Enter a Gemini key, pick a model and set up Health Connect.

**Prerequisites:** For the key: a Google AI Studio account.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**. Paste the key or scan it from a QR code, pick a model and tap **Save key**. **Test connection** checks the key and model. The link below leads to Google AI Studio for a free key.

   ![Settings: Gemini key and Health Connect – 1](../../media/10-language-and-settings/en-01-settings.png)

2. Further down: **Health Connect** writes the food you add for other apps and can adjust the goal by burned calories.

   ![Settings: Gemini key and Health Connect – 2](../../media/10-language-and-settings/en-02-settings-more.png)


## Result

Without a key the app works with the offline labels only. The key is stored only on the phone.
