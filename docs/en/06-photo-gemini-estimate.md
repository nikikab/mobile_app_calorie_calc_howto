# 6. Food photo: Gemini estimate

Gemini estimates the meal from the photo: name, grams, calories and macros.

**Prerequisites:** A Gemini API key entered in Settings (see scenario 10). The photo is sent to Google on every shot while a key is set.

## Steps

1. Take a photo of the food or choose one.
2. After a few seconds cards with estimates appear under the photo (one per food).
3. Tap the green **Add** button on a card. The entry goes into the selected meal as an estimate.

## Result

Estimates are approximate. If something is off, fix it with the next scenario.
