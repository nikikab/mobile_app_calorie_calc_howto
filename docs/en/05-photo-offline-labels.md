# 5. Food photo: offline labels

Snap the meal and the app suggests search terms with no internet and no key.

**Prerequisites:** Camera or gallery access.

## Steps

1. Tap **Take food photo** (or **Choose photo** from **+**).
2. On-device recognition (ML Kit) suggests generic English labels such as "Pizza". Tap a label to search with it.
3. Pick a product and grams as in a normal search. The photo is saved with the entry.

## Result

The labels are generic, not an exact dish. For name, grams and calories see the next scenario.
