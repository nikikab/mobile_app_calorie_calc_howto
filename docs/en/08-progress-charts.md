# 8. Progress: weight and calories

Log your weight and review calories for the last 7 days.

**Prerequisites:** None.

## Steps

1. Open the **Progress** tab. Enter today's weight and tap **Log**. The line chart shows the change.

   ![Progress: weight and calories – 1](../../media/08-progress-charts/en-01-charts.png)

2. The bars below are calories for the last 7 days and the line is your daily goal.

## Result

The goal in the Goal tab follows the logged weight.
